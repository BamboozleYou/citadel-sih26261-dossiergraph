# Past SIH Problem Statements and Solutions Similar to SIH26261 (AICTE AI-powered application validation / document authentication)

Research date: 2026-10-26. sih.gov.in problem-statement and result pages returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches (Azure gateway block), so official per-PS submission counts and official winner tables could NOT be read directly. PS lists below come from college-hosted copies of the official lists, which should be treated as secondary sources.

## 1. Which past SIH problem statements (2019–2025) are comparable?

### Takeaway
AICTE has posted closely related PSs before. SIH 2024 had **SIH1729 "AI supported AICTE Approval process portal"** and **SIH1730 "AI driven Inspection of Institutions"**, both AICTE, Software, Smart Automation. Together these two cover most of SIH26261's scope (approval-application scrutiny plus infrastructure/inspection verification). Academic-credential forgery also has a lineage: SIH 2019 "Trustee" (a blockchain certificate store that won first prize) and SIH 2025 **SIH25029 "Authenticity Validator for Academia"** (Govt. of Jharkhand).

### Inferences
- SIH26261 most closely resembles SIH1729 + SIH1730 (2024) merged, with the credential-forgery component of SIH25029 (2025) added. For SIH 2026, AICTE appears to have split the approval domain into several PSs: 26261 (validation/authentication/compliance), 26262 (workflow/alerts), 26265 (FDR). Judges will probably expect 26261 to focus on document intelligence and compliance rather than on workflow.

- The full official problem text of SIH1729/SIH1730 could not be read (sih.gov.in 403).

## 2. Is SIH26261 new in SIH 2026 or a repeat?

### Takeaway
The PS ID SIH26261 is new to SIH 2026. Its title, as quoted by teams, is "AI-Powered Application Validation, Document Authentication and Regulatory Compliance Verification". It is not a verbatim repeat, but it is a clear evolution of 2024's SIH1729/SIH1730 AICTE PSs. I could not access its official page on sih.gov.in.

### Cited Findings
- The title quoted in a team README: "AI-Powered Application Validation, Document Authentication and Regulatory Compliance Verification", SIH 2026, PS 26261. [amanray10/Nirikshan-SIH26261](https://github.com/amanray10/Nirikshan-SIH26261)
- Teams frame it as AICTE Extension of Approval (EoA) / approval-dossier scrutiny against the Approval Process Handbook (APH 2024–27). [atharva23x/verigate](https://github.com/atharva23x/verigate); [aquasimp/SIH26261](https://github.com/aquasimp/SIH26261)
- SIH 2026 launched 21 Aug 2026. A third-party blog reports 226 PSs in the first lot (172 software, 54 hardware); that figure is secondary and unverified. [Reskilll blog](https://blogs.reskilll.com/sih-2026-problem-statements-released-categories-themes-early-analysis/); search summary of the same blog.
- The SIH 2026 guidelines (IGNOU copy) list sector areas such as Smart Education, but give no PS-specific detail. [IGNOU SIH 2026 Guidelines](https://www.ignou.ac.in/viewFile/NCIDE/notification/Guidelines-SIH-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Because AICTE re-posted the approval/inspection theme after 2024, the 2024 solutions apparently did not satisfy AICTE's need, or AICTE wants a deeper document-forensics angle this year. Pitches that cite the real APH and the National Single Window System (NSWS) workflow may carry weight; the official APH states that applications go online through NSWS/AICTE portal only. [AICTE APH 2024-27 PDF](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s35938b4d054136e5d59ada6ec9c295d7a/uploads/2025/03/2025031399.pdf)

### Gaps
- Category/theme of SIH26261 and its live submission counter were not visible (sih.gov.in blocked).

## 3. What did teams build for these PSs (past and current)?

### Takeaway
Past credential-verification solutions are mostly **hash/blockchain + QR + OCR + DB match**. Current SIH26261 public repos have moved upmarket. Several already claim **APH-rules-as-code, ELA/noise forensics, digital-signature (PAdES) checks, issuer-registry connectors, perceptual-hash photo reuse detection, ghost-faculty cross-institution checks, and evidence-linked officer workstations with a "human decides" stance**. A sophisticated idea is therefore not automatically differentiated this year.

### Cited Findings
**Past (credential forgery):**
- SIH 2019 winner "Trustee": blockchain certificate storage. [Chitkara](https://chitkara.edu.in/news/chitkara-university-team-gully-gang-bag-first-prize-at-sih-2019)
- SIH25029 CertiCheck: Ed25519-signed QR + Tesseract OCR + university records DB with fuzzy name match → Genuine/Suspicious/Fake with reasons (~1.3 s claimed). [certicheck-sih25029](https://github.com/Krishna945-crtl/certicheck-sih25029)
- SIH25029 CertAuth: React/Node/Express/MongoDB, SHA-256 hashing, QR verification, JWT, email OTP. [Shreeyoga/certauth](https://github.com/Shreeyoga/certauth)
- An "Academic Authenticity Validator" paper (HITAM, Telangana, IRJET Sept 2026) uses SHA-256 hashes stored via a Solidity smart contract and React/Node/Express/MongoDB. Possibly an SIH write-up (unconfirmed). [IRJET V13I0948](https://www.irjet.net/archives/V13/i9/IRJET-V13I0948.pdf)
- A student on the "Authenticity Validator for Academia" PS described a pHash pipeline plus exploration of ELA and CNN (from a search-result summary of a LinkedIn post). [LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/in/ankitarsh19)
- SIH1728 deck: React/Node/Django, AWS, Tableau, UIDAI APIs, AI chatbot, automated doc verification. [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/sih-presentation-problem-statement-sih-1728/280871980)
- A forgery attack on a blockchain credential system (Block.co) used a Merkle proof and an address exposed on public GitHub. This weakens "blockchain = unforgeable" pitches. [arXiv 2606.31462](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31462)

**Current SIH26261 public repos (prototypes, not results):**
- Nirikshan: 14 APH rules as unit-tested Python with clause citations (FSR 1:20, cadre 1:2:6, built-up area, lease ≥30/25 yrs, fire NOC); four-level document authentication (metadata, noise-inconsistency forensics, pyHanko digital signatures/QR, issuer registry mock); Tesseract/PaddleOCR; BGE-M3+FAISS clause retrieval; local LLM via Ollama; ghost-faculty salted-hash cross-institution checks; perceptual-hash recycled-photo detection; year-over-year consistency; institution self-check mode. [amanray10/Nirikshan-SIH26261](https://github.com/amanray10/Nirikshan-SIH26261)
- AICTE Regulatory Scrutiny & Forensic Document Intelligence Workstation: Next.js 15 / React 19, ELA residuals, Noiseprint++ heatmaps, APH rule citations, "DPI" cross-checks, live Vercel deployment. [aquasimp/SIH26261](https://github.com/aquasimp/SIH26261)
- PRAMAAN: rule pack + AICTE records, issuing-office confirmation, edit detection, infrastructure photo checks, officer-decides; LITE (CPU) and FULL (GPU, five models) modes; LangGraph; MongoDB; live demo. [Adi-ctive/pramaan](https://github.com/Adi-ctive/pramaan)
- EduVerify AI: FastAPI + React; deterministic eligibility rules, cross-document consistency, human review/audit trail (interprets the PS as student admissions, which is a different reading). [Mukhesh-san/eduverify-ai](https://github.com/Mukhesh-san/eduverify-ai)
- Other titled repos: "AI-powered document forensics and regulatory compliance gateway" [Dhapoo06](https://github.com/Dhapoo06/SIH-Team-kratoseyal-prototype); VeriGate for EoA [atharva23x/verigate](https://github.com/atharva23x/verigate); AICTE-VERITAS [navyaanubhutii/AICTE-VERITAS](https://github.com/navyaanubhutii/AICTE-VERITAS); regulatory extraction + deterministic validation pipeline [ShreyashS1107/datasetSIH26261](https://github.com/ShreyashS1107/datasetSIH26261); SatyaPramaan, DocuLens, PramaanAI (names only).

### Inferences
- The 2026 field has converged on one vocabulary: "evidence-linked", "officer decides", "APH as code", "ELA/forensics", "issuer verification". Differentiation must come from depth and demonstrable accuracy (real APH clauses, measured forensic precision on a labelled test set, a working issuer-verification flow such as DigiLocker/NAD/PAdES), not from the feature list.
- Name collisions are likely (at least three teams use "Pramaan").

One ABESIT LinkedIn post claims a Top-5/Grand-Finale place on SIH25029 but says the trophy was not won; its wording is internally contradictory. [LinkedIn – Avdhesh Kumar Tiwari](https://www.linkedin.com/in/avdhesh-kumar-tiwari-0b6a1a1a9/). The SIH25029 nodal centre was reportedly IIT (ISM) Dhanbad. [Medium (403 on fetch; from search summary)](https://medium.com/@vidyajaggi05/sih25-a-journey-to-look-back-99ed087c47db)

## 4. What is the common baseline, and what made winners stand out?

### Takeaway
The historical baseline for verification PSs is **OCR (Tesseract) → field extraction → hash/QR or blockchain anchor → DB lookup → admin dashboard**, usually on React/Node/MongoDB. Blockchain was a winning differentiator in 2019 and still appeared in a 2025 credential PS win. By SIH 2025–2026 it is commodity. The 2026 SIH26261 baseline among visible repos is already higher: OCR + LLM/RAG over the APH + rule engine + ELA-style image forensics + dashboard with audit trail.

### Cited Findings
- Hash/QR/OCR/DB pattern: [certicheck-sih25029](https://github.com/Krishna945-crtl/certicheck-sih25029), [certauth](https://github.com/Shreeyoga/certauth), [IRJET paper](https://www.irjet.net/archives/V13/i9/IRJET-V13I0948.pdf)
- Blockchain winners: SIH 2019 Trustee [Chitkara](https://chitkara.edu.in/news/chitkara-university-team-gully-gang-bag-first-prize-at-sih-2019); SIH25200 (self-reported) [LinkedIn summary](https://in.linkedin.com/in/sriram484)
- Academic OCR + ResNet forgery detector prototype on Indian certificates reported only 78% accuracy on 200 certificates. This suggests ML-only forgery claims are weak without good data. (Search-result summary; paper URL not retrieved; treat as unverified.)
- Third-party advice for SIH 2026: AI integration is expected in most solutions, offline capability may score higher, and a working video demo is mandatory. [Reskilll blog](https://blogs.reskilll.com/sih-2026-problem-statements-released-categories-themes-early-analysis/)
- SIH26261 repos converge on ELA/forensics + APH rules + officer workstation: [Nirikshan](https://github.com/amanray10/Nirikshan-SIH26261), [aquasimp](https://github.com/aquasimp/SIH26261), [PRAMAAN](https://github.com/Adi-ctive/pramaan)

### Inferences
- Pitching "OCR + blockchain + QR" for SIH26261 would sit at or below the baseline. It also mismatches the PS: AICTE receives already-issued third-party documents (land deeds, NOCs, building plans, faculty certificates), so issuer-side QR/blockchain cannot verify legacy documents.
- Likely winner traits, based on what past winners emphasised (speed, working demo, issuer-side trust) plus current field saturation: a live end-to-end demo on realistic APH dossiers, explicit APH clause mapping, measurable forensic results, a credible integration story (DigiLocker/NAD/APAAR, eSign/PAdES, state land records), and human-in-the-loop explainability. This is an inference, not documented jury criteria.

### Gaps
- No published jury feedback for SIH1729/1730/25029 was found.

## 5. How competitive are AICTE/MoE PSs?

### Takeaway
Education-domain PSs are among the most crowded. SIH 2025 capped each PS at 500 idea submissions and froze it once the cap was reached. SIH26261 already shows heavy visible activity: 23 GitHub repos match "SIH26261" (77 match "26261"), most created in the last two weeks before 26 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- SIH 2025: max 500 ideas per PS, then the PS freezes; ideas-submitted and ideas-left counts were shown publicly; at most 2 PSs per team (college guideline copy). [VSSUT SIH 2025 doc](https://vssut.ac.in/images/documents/docs_1756739827.pdf)
- A student on "Authenticity Validator for Academia" reported the PS was already full when they reached that stage and switched PS (search summary of LinkedIn post). This suggests the credential-forgery PS hit the cap. [LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/in/ankitarsh19)
- Third-party estimate (unsourced): Education/Healthcare/Smart Tech PSs attract 500+ teams each. [Reskilll blog](https://blogs.reskilll.com/sih-2026-problem-statements-released-categories-themes-early-analysis/)
- GitHub search counts run by me on 2026-10-26 with `gh search repos`: "SIH26261" gives 23 repos, "26261" gives 77. Compare SIH26262 (≥5) and SIH26265 (≥5, limited query). Source: GitHub search (e.g. [github.com/search?q=SIH26261](https://github.com/search?q=SIH26261&type=repositories))

### Inferences
- Public repos are only a small fraction of teams, so SIH26261 likely has hundreds of internal/idea submissions, possibly near a cap if 2026 retains the 500 rule. Typically only a handful of teams per PS are shortlisted for the Grand Finale (historically about 3–5 per PS; unverified for 2026).

### Gaps
- The official live submission count for SIH26261 was not retrievable (sih.gov.in 403). Check it manually on the PS page.
- I found no data on finalist counts per PS for 2026.
