# How Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Ideas Are Evaluated: Internal Round to Grand Finale (focus SIH 2024–2026)

Research note: sih.gov.in returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches, so official-portal claims below come via press releases (PIB / All India Radio newsonair), college notices, and copies of the official template. Several 2026-specific details come only from third-party blogs (Reskilll, FirstVidya) and are labelled as such. Research date: 26 Oct 2026.

## 1. Official SIH process and timelines (internal hackathon, portal submission, PS-owner evaluation, grand finale)

### Takeaway
SIH runs in four stages: college SPOC registration, then the institute internal hackathon (September), then SPOC nomination of up to ~50 teams with their idea PPTs on the portal (deadline end-September), then expert/PS-owner screening and shortlisting (Oct–Nov), then a 36-hour software Grand Finale at nodal centres in December. SIH 2025 drew 72,165 idea submissions for 271 PSs, and 1,360 teams reached the finale. For SIH 2026, third-party sources report 226 PSs in the first lot (172 software) and a December 2026 finale. The official 2026 portal dates could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
**Stage rules (institute level)**
- The college SPOC nominates top teams based on internal hackathon results, up to 50 teams per institute. Teams are 6 students from the same college including at least one female member, and only internal-hackathon selectees can be registered nationally — [MAIT SIH internal 2025](https://cse.mait.ac.in/index.php/9-computer-center/1327-smart-india-internal-hackathon-2025); [Sri Venkateswara College notice](https://www.svc.ac.in/kmcouter/collnews/download/62971549175876862440.pdf/College%20News/pdf/All/All)
- Nomination limits are reported as up to 50 teams per college (45 shortlisted + 5 waitlisted) and up to 100 per university. Each PS reportedly locks after 500 idea submissions, and a team can enter up to 2 PSs (third-party, unverified) — [FirstVidya SIH 2026 guide](https://firstvidya.com/sih-2026-guide/)
- Example SIH 2025 internal-round timelines: IIIT Bhopal registration 26 Aug, PPT 14 Sep, internal event 19 Sep 2025 — [IEEE vTools](https://events.vtools.ieee.org/m/499274). Sri Venkateswara College held its internal round on 26 Sep, named nominees on 28 Sep, and the SPOC entered them on the portal 28–30 Sep 2025 — [SVC notice](https://www.svc.ac.in/kmcouter/collnews/download/62971549175876862440.pdf/College%20News/pdf/All/All)
- MAIT's 2025 internal round had 247 registered teams. 168 were shortlisted on presentations, judged on idea innovation, solution to the PS and teamwork — [MAIT](https://cse.mait.ac.in/index.php/9-computer-center/1327-smart-india-internal-hackathon-2025)

**SIH 2024 (7th edition)**
- More than 86,000 teams took part at institute level, and about 49,000 teams were recommended by institutes for the national round. More than 250 PSs came from 54 ministries, departments, states, PSUs and industries, plus a Student Innovation category across 17 themes. About 2,247 institute-level hackathons were held, up from about 900 in 2023 — [newsonair, 9 Dec 2024](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/pm-modi-to-interact-with-young-innovators-at-grand-finale-of-smart-india-hackathon-2024); [narendramodi.in](https://narendramodi.in/pm-to-interact-with-participants-of-smart-india-hackathon-2024-on-11th-december)
- The finale started on 11 Dec 2024 at 51 nodal centres. The software edition was a nonstop 36-hour event, and the hardware edition ran 11–15 Dec. More than 1,300 teams were projected (pre-event figure) — [narendramodi.in](https://narendramodi.in/pm-to-interact-with-participants-of-smart-india-hackathon-2024-on-11th-december)
- The reported growth in internal hackathons conflicts: one release says 150%, while an MoE PIB release reportedly says 240% (search-snippet level) — [newsonair](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/pm-modi-to-interact-with-young-innovators-at-grand-finale-of-smart-india-hackathon-2024); [education.gov.in PIB](https://education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/PIB2081437.pdf)

**SIH 2025 (8th edition)**
- There were 72,165 idea submissions from 68,766 teams against 271 PSs from ministries, state departments, PSUs and industry. 1,360 teams (more than 8,000 students and more than 1,300 mentors) qualified for the finale at 60 nodal centres. The software finale ran 8–9 Dec 2025, with WeSchool Mumbai named as a venue — [newsonair, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/8th-edition-of-smart-india-hackathon-2025-begins-mic)
- The 271 PSs reportedly split into 191 software and 80 hardware. The hardware finale ran at IIT Kharagpur on 8–12 Dec 2025. Internal hackathons covered 2,587 institutions, 1,42,715 teams and 8,26,635 students (search-result summary of official figures) — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/iit-kharagpur-hosts-sih-2025-hardware-grand-finale-platinum-jubilee-smart-india-hackathon-education-ministry-aicte-innovation-cell)

**SIH 2026 (9th edition)** (third-party sources unless noted)
- SIH 2026 was reportedly launched on 21 Aug 2026 by MIC and AICTE. The reported sequence is: August, SPOC registration and PS release; September, internal hackathons; October–November, "online evaluation, pre-screening of idea PPTs, shortlisting of national finalists"; December, a 36-hour Grand Finale at nodal centres. The SPOC uploads the idea PPT and a video demonstration — [Reskilll](https://blogs.reskilll.com/smart-india-hackathon-2026-launched-timeline-registration-how-to-participate/)
- The first lot reportedly has 226 PSs (172 software, 54 hardware), released to institutions from 25 Aug 2026 — [Reskilll PS post](https://blogs.reskilll.com/?p=236)
- The nomination and idea-submission deadline is reported as 30 September, a "hard deadline" — [FirstVidya](https://firstvidya.com/sih-2026-guide/)
- College pages confirm the timing. BCREC Durgapur closed team registration on 7 Sep 2026, took ideas until 11 Sep and lists the finale as "proposed December 2026 (offline at nodal centres)". NEHU held its internal round on 7 Sep 2026, and IET DAVV on 12 Sep 2026 — [BCREC](https://bcrec.ac.in/sih); [NEHU](https://nehu.ac.in/event/671); [IET DAVV](https://sih.ietdavv.edu.in/)

**Finale structure and prize**
- The finale alternates mentoring and evaluation rounds and ends with a final pitch and judging — [Kartikay Singh, SIH 2025 winner, Medium](https://medium.com/@kartikaysingh4329/how-i-won-indias-biggest-hackathon-sih-2025-69dafd1d792e) (search snippet; the page returned 403 on fetch). In SIH 2022, evaluation started on the evening of day 1 with 3-judge panels, followed by a second mentoring round and another evaluation the next morning — [Sumathi Vidhya, Medium](https://sumathividhya.medium.com/smart-india-hackathon-22-668f70531bbc)
- One winner per PS. Some PSs may have no winner. (Labelled older: SIH 2019 student FAQ and SIH 2020 document) — [SIH Student FAQs](https://sih.gov.in/letters/Student%20FAQs.pdf); [RVRJCCE SIH 2020](https://rvrjcce.ac.in/SIH%202020.pdf)
- Prize is ₹1,50,000 per PS winning team (third-party, 2026) — [FirstVidya](https://firstvidya.com/sih-2026-guide/)

### Inferences
- Nationally, about 1 in 50 submitted ideas reaches the finale (SIH 2025: 1,360 finalists out of 72,165 submissions, about 1.9%). The internal hackathon is a further filter before that (1,42,715 internal teams became 68,766 national submitters, roughly 48%).
- For 2026, the idea PPT has already been submitted (late September). Screening and shortlisting fall in October–November 2026, so as of 26 Oct 2026 results are likely pending or imminent.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 dates from sih.gov.in (403 on fetch). Whether the 2026 software finale is 1 or 2 days and its exact dates are unconfirmed.
- No post-event official count of SIH 2024 finalists or total ideas submitted.
- The official evaluator workflow (who screens: PS-owner ministries versus MIC-appointed experts) is not documented in an accessible official source. The "500 submissions per PS cap" is third-party only.

## 2. Official idea-submission PPT template

### Takeaway
The official template, near-identical across 2024, 2025 and 2026, has 6 slides maximum including the title slide, must be submitted as PDF only, and uses fixed slide headings: Title, Idea/Proposed Solution, Technical Approach, Feasibility and Viability, Impact and Benefits, Research and References. Third-party "10-slide" advice contradicts the official cap.

### Cited Findings
- SIH 2025 template copy, slide by slide — [Google Slides copy of SIH 2025 template](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1UAcsy0OUn86ZzrcGbXTdcyoc6uqV4Jr2/htmlpresent):
  - Slide 1, Title: PS ID, PS Title, Theme, PS Category (Software), Team ID, Team Name (as registered on the portal).
  - Slide 2, Idea Title / Proposed Solution: detailed explanation, how it addresses the problem, innovation and uniqueness.
  - Slide 3, Technical Approach: technologies (languages, frameworks, hardware) and methodology/implementation process via flowcharts, images or a working prototype.
  - Slide 4, Feasibility and Viability: feasibility analysis, potential challenges and risks, strategies to overcome them.
  - Slide 5, Impact and Benefits: impact on the target audience, and social, economic and environmental benefits.
  - Slide 6, Research and References: details and links of references and research work.
  - Instructions (delete before upload): max 6 slides including title; points, diagrams, infographics and pictures rather than paragraphs; precise; "idea must be unique and novel"; do not change the pointers; PDF only, with PPT and Word not accepted.
- The 2024, 2025 and 2026 copies are nearly identical. The 2025 copy lists the PS category as "Software", while 2024 and 2026 say "Software/Hardware" — search summary of [template copies](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1gPowH1jbmcuv3vksVzNv9dee5pHujWWc/htmlpresent); [SlideShare SIH 2024 PPT](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/sih2024-ppt-presentation-for-smart-india-hackathon/271778038)
- Contradiction: Reskilll's "SIH 2026 PPT Template" post recommends up to 10 slides (Problem Understanding, Innovation, Prototype, 36-hour Timeline, Team) and a file under 10 MB. It cites no official source — [Reskilll](https://blogs.reskilll.com/sih-2026-ppt-template-exact-format-slides-evaluators-score/)
- For 2026, the SPOC reportedly uploads an idea PPT plus a video demonstration — [Reskilll](https://blogs.reskilll.com/smart-india-hackathon-2026-launched-timeline-registration-how-to-participate/)

### Inferences
- The slide headings work as the de facto rubric. Evaluators look for innovation and uniqueness, technical approach, feasibility and risk, impact, and references. An idea with no credible risk-mitigation or reference evidence leaves a mandatory slide weak.
- Prefer the official 6-slide template over any 10-slide advice.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm from an official 2026 source whether a video is mandatory, or its length.

## 3. Evaluation criteria / rubric and weightings

### Takeaway
No official national rubric with weights was found publicly. Every reported rubric, whether from colleges, blogs or the template itself, converges on novelty/innovation, feasibility, technical approach/complexity, impact/scalability, problem-fit and clarity, and presentation. Weightings exist only at college level or in unsourced blogs.

### Cited Findings
- Unsourced blog weights: Innovation/uniqueness 25%, Problem understanding 20%, Technical feasibility 20%, Impact/scalability 20%, Presentation 15%. The same post claims "50,000+ teams submit PPTs. Evaluators spend 2-3 minutes per submission". Neither claim is backed by an official source — [Reskilll](https://blogs.reskilll.com/sih-2026-ppt-template-exact-format-slides-evaluators-score/)
- National-stage experts reportedly score novelty, feasibility, impact and clarity, and juries score against the PS's "expected solution" (third-party) — [FirstVidya](https://firstvidya.com/sih-2026-guide/)
- College internal rubric examples:
  - Innovativeness 20%, technical approach 30%, feasibility/scalability 20%, with the rest on presentation and related areas — [SSIP Gujarat report](https://www.ssipgujarat.in/admin20/files/event/report1_path/6991/65_3.pdf)
  - Novelty 15/100 and feasibility 20/100 (polytechnic), and a ten-criterion list including complexity, sustainability, scale of impact and future progression — [SSIP Gujarat Internal Hackathon Report 2025](https://ssipgujarat.in/admin20/files/event/report1_path/8620/Internal%20Hackathon%20Report%202025.pdf)
  - Problem clarity, novelty, feasibility — [NNRG Internal SIH 2025](https://nnrg.edu.in/PDF/events/cse/Internal-SIH-2025.pdf)
  - These are search-result summaries of the PDFs.
- SIH 2023 internal (labelled 2023): idea and clarity, implementation prospect, presentation skill, problem-solving weightage, team skills — [MCKVIE SIH Report 2023](https://www.mckvie.edu.in/pdf/SMART%20INDIA%20HACKATHON%20Report%202023.pdf)
- MAIT 2025 internal: idea innovation, solution to the PS, teamwork — [MAIT](https://cse.mait.ac.in/index.php/9-computer-center/1327-smart-india-internal-hackathon-2025)
- At the finale, judges hand out improvement and feature lists after round 2, and how a team responds to feedback across rounds matters. A 2023 team struggled in round 1 because its prototype was not integrated, and recovered in later rounds — [Sarika Purohit, SIH 2023 winner](https://sarika-purohit.medium.com/my-smart-india-hackathon-sih2023-journey-as-team-lead-winner-63bc00b3cd34); [Vansh Rathi, SIH 2023](https://medium.com/@vkrvanshrathi/sih23-the-road-to-the-grand-finale-of-smart-india-hackathon-25869c133734)

### Inferences
- A practical scoring frame, consistent with all sources: Novelty/uniqueness ~20–25%, PS-fit/problem understanding ~20%, Technical approach and feasibility ~20–30%, Impact/scalability ~20%, Presentation/clarity ~15%. Treat these weights as indicative only.
- Fit with the PS owner's "expected solution" is reported as a key scoring anchor at national level, because PS-owning organisations decide whether a solution meets their expectations (one winner per PS, possibly none).

### Gaps
- No official MIC/AICTE national rubric or weighting document found. No official confirmation of the per-PPT evaluation time.

## 4. Shortlisting numbers: teams per PS and ideas per PS

### Takeaway
On average, popular-PS competition runs to hundreds of ideas per PS and roughly 5 finalists per PS. SIH 2025 had about 266 ideas per PS on average (72,165/271) and about 5.0 finalist teams per PS (1,360/271). No official per-PS cap on finalists was found, and third-party sources say about 4–5.

### Cited Findings
- SIH 2025: 72,165 ideas, 271 PSs, 1,360 finalist teams — [newsonair](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/8th-edition-of-smart-india-hackathon-2025-begins-mic)
- About 4–5 teams per PS are selected for the finale, and each PS locks at 500 submissions (third-party) — [FirstVidya](https://firstvidya.com/sih-2026-guide/)
- SIH 2022 software (labelled older), ISRO: 22 PSs, 36 teams shortlisted (about 1.6 per PS), 32 attended, finale at 75 nodal centres — [ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO_Organizes_Smart.html)
- SIH 2018 (labelled older): 1,296 entries selected for the finale across 340 software problems (about 3.8 per PS) — search summary of [Amar Ujala](https://results.amarujala.com/career-diary/grand-finale-of-smart-india-hackathon-2018-software-edition-to-be-held-on-march-30-and-31)
- SIH 2017 (labelled older): a college reported the top ~10–15% of 7,531 submissions would be shortlisted — search-result summary of college reports (the source URL could not be pinned down, so treat with caution)
- SIH 2024: about 49,000 teams nominated nationally, 250+ PSs plus a 17-theme Student Innovation category, and about 1,300 finalist teams projected — [newsonair](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/pm-modi-to-interact-with-young-innovators-at-grand-finale-of-smart-india-hackathon-2024)

### Inferences
- Selection rate is roughly 2% overall in 2025. In 2024, roughly 1,300 of up to ~49,000 nominated teams made the finale (about 2.7%). The per-PS figure varies widely: ISRO in 2022 shortlisted fewer than 2 per PS, while the averages imply about 4–5. Popular, generic-sounding PSs likely see several hundred entries for about 5 slots, so niche PSs may offer better odds.
- The 2025 average includes hardware PSs, and the software/hardware finalist split was not found.

### Gaps
- No official per-PS submission counts or per-PS finalist caps for 2024–2026. The portal may show per-PS counts, but it was not accessible.

## 5. What makes ideas get shortlisted vs rejected (winners, mentors, judges)

### Takeaway
The consistent failure modes reported are:
- not solving the exact PS, or misreading it;
- generic or copied ideas (including past SIH solutions);
- vague tech ("we'll use AI") or buzzword AI/blockchain;
- weak feasibility;
- rushed or generic decks;
- rule and format violations.

Winners stress a deck that stands on its own at screening, a working and integrated prototype at the finale, and responding to judge feedback. Most of this evidence is from blogs and winner accounts, not official judges.

### Cited Findings
- Misreading the PS is called the top killer, and teams should test whether the organisation would actually use the result. Copying past SIH solutions, missing the female-member rule and late submission also eliminate teams — [Reskilll common mistakes](https://blogs.reskilll.com/common-sih-mistakes-teams-eliminated-how-to-avoid/); [Reskilll PPT post](https://blogs.reskilll.com/sih-2026-ppt-template-exact-format-slides-evaluators-score/)
- "AI chatbot for X" and "Blockchain for Y" are named as ideas judges are tired of, with blockchain called over-engineered for 36 hours — [Reskilll "ideas that won"](https://blogs.reskilll.com/hackathon-ideas-that-won-2026-judges-actually-pick-examples/)
- A rushed, generic write-up rarely survives the college shortlist, and the internal stage is where many teams drop out — [Velonx first-timer's guide](https://velonx.in/blog/sih-2026-first-timers-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-before-you-register)
- At national screening the deck is reviewed without the team present, so it must carry the idea alone. A 2025 winner called this stage partly luck — [Kartikay Singh, Medium](https://medium.com/@kartikaysingh4329/how-i-won-indias-biggest-hackathon-sih-2025-69dafd1d792e) (via search snippet)
- The official template's own instruction says "idea must be unique and novel", with points and diagrams rather than paragraphs — [SIH 2025 template copy](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1UAcsy0OUn86ZzrcGbXTdcyoc6uqV4Jr2/htmlpresent)
- A prototype "dramatically improves" chances but is not a formal requirement at the idea stage (third-party) — [FirstVidya](https://firstvidya.com/sih-2026-guide/)
- At the finale, a non-integrated prototype hurt round-1 evaluation, and judges' improvement lists must be acted on — [Vansh Rathi](https://medium.com/@vkrvanshrathi/sih23-the-road-to-the-grand-finale-of-smart-india-hackathon-25869c133734); [Sarika Purohit](https://sarika-purohit.medium.com/my-smart-india-hackathon-sih2023-journey-as-team-lead-winner-63bc00b3cd34)
- Administrative failures include wrong email IDs, outdated PPT templates and copy-pasted content — search summary of a YouTube video page (low reliability)

### Inferences
- To rate an idea, the key checks are: (a) it maps line by line to the PS description and expected outcome; (b) one concrete differentiator against existing solutions; (c) a named tech stack with an architecture or flow diagram, not buzzwords; (d) explicit risks and mitigations; (e) quantified beneficiaries and impact; (f) credible references; (g) an MVP buildable in 36 hours.

### Gaps
- No first-hand published statements from official SIH evaluators or PS-owner ministries on rejection reasons. No Reddit or LinkedIn threads were retrieved.

## 6. Do judges value security/privacy as a differentiator?

### Takeaway
There is no evidence that security or privacy is a formal SIH scoring criterion; it is absent from every rubric found. There is indirect evidence that it matters when the PS involves sensitive data: several past SIH winners (2017–2019, older) were security-centric solutions for government PS owners such as Defence and MEA.

### Cited Findings
- SIH 2019 (older) winner: Team Kalam's Dream (RMK Engineering College) built a secure data-transfer portal using digital signatures and SSL, and won ₹1 lakh — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2019/07/data-security-system-smart-india-hackathon-student)
- SIH 2018 (older): Amrita's "Cyber Sena" won with a secure USB/data-protection system for the Ministry of Defence — [Amrita](https://www.amrita.edu/news/cyber-sena-of-amrita-wins-top-award-at-smart-india-hackathon-2018)
- SIH 2017 (older): a Chitkara team placed in the top six with end-to-end email encryption for MEA — [Chitkara](https://www.chitkara.edu.in/?p=21917)
- A 2023 SIH college rubric does not list security — [MCKVIE 2023](https://www.mckvie.edu.in/pdf/SMART%20INDIA%20HACKATHON%20Report%202023.pdf)
- Contrast: the RBI hackathon (not SIH) explicitly scored security alongside innovation, comprehensiveness, product readiness and scalability — [RBI press release](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=62590)

### Inferences
- Security and privacy are best framed inside existing criteria: feasibility/risks (slide 4) and impact/trust (slide 5). This applies especially for government PS owners handling citizen, health, financial or defence data, where it can differentiate an idea from generic competitors. It is unlikely to rescue an idea that misses PS-fit or novelty.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 evidence found of judges explicitly rewarding security/privacy, and no judge quotes on it.
